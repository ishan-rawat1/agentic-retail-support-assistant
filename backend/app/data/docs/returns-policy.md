# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return windows

- **Standard items** may be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund.
- **Electronics** (headphones, smart lamps, and other powered devices) may be returned
  within **15 days of delivery** due to rapid product cycles.
- **Final sale and clearance items** are **not returnable** and are clearly marked
  "Final Sale" on the product page and receipt.
- **Gift cards** are **non-returnable and non-refundable** in all cases.

The return window is measured from the **delivery date**, not the order date. Items
that have not yet been delivered cannot be returned; if your order has not shipped
you may cancel it instead (see the Payments & Cancellations policy).

## Condition requirements

Returned items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories.
Footwear must be unworn outdoors. Items showing wear beyond inspection may receive
a partial refund of up to 50% at our discretion.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in and open **Orders**, or ask our support assistant to check eligibility.
2. Select the item and reason; a prepaid return label is emailed within 1 hour.
3. Drop the package at any carrier location within 7 days of label creation.

## Refund processing

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method within 5–7 business days** after
the warehouse receives and inspects the item. Shipping fees are refundable only when
the return is due to our error (wrong, damaged, or defective item).

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place
a new order.
