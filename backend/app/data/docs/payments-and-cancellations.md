# Payments & Cancellations Policy

## Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay,
Google Pay, and our own gift cards. Up to two payment methods can be combined
per order (for example, a gift card plus a credit card).

## When you are charged

Your payment method is **authorized at checkout and charged when the order
ships**. Gift cards are charged immediately at checkout.

## Cancelling an order

Orders can be cancelled free of charge **any time before they ship** — while the
order status is "processing". Once an order has shipped it can no longer be
cancelled, but you can return it after delivery under the Returns & Refunds
Policy. To cancel, open **Orders** and choose "Cancel order", or contact support
with your order number.

Cancelled orders are never charged; any authorization hold is released within
3–5 business days depending on your bank.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are delivered by email within minutes of purchase, never expire, and
carry no fees. Gift cards cannot be returned, refunded, or redeemed for cash
except where required by law.

## Price adjustments

If an item you purchased drops in price within **7 days** of your order date, we
refund the difference to your original payment method on request.
