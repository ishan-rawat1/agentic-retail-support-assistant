# Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Shipping methods and timelines

| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $4.99 (free on orders over $50) |
| Express | 2 business days | $12.99 |
| Overnight | Next business day (order by 2 PM ET) | $24.99 |

Orders are processed within **1 business day**. Orders placed on weekends or
holidays begin processing the next business day.

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is **free for all orders over $50** before tax. Express and
overnight shipping are never free, including for orders over $50.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed as soon as your order ships. You can also ask the
support assistant "Where is my order?" with your order number (for example,
ORD-1001) to get live status, carrier, and estimated delivery date.

## Shipping destinations

We currently ship to all 50 US states. We do **not** ship internationally or to
PO boxes for items over 20 lb (such as standing desks).

## Delayed or lost packages

If a package shows no tracking movement for 5 business days, contact support and
we will open a carrier investigation. Packages confirmed lost are replaced or
refunded in full within 2 business days of the carrier's confirmation.

## Damaged on arrival

Report damaged items within **48 hours of delivery** with a photo. We ship a
replacement immediately at no cost; the damaged item usually does not need to be
returned.
