# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?

Ask the support assistant "Where is my order ORD-XXXX?" or open **Orders** in
your account. Once an order ships you receive a tracking number by email, and
the assistant can show live status, carrier, and the estimated delivery date.

## Can I change my shipping address after ordering?

Yes, while the order status is still "processing". Contact support as soon as
possible. Once an order has shipped the address cannot be changed, but you can
contact the carrier to request a hold at a pickup location.

## Do you ship internationally?

No. We currently ship only within the 50 US states.

## How long do refunds take?

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within **5–7 business days**
after the warehouse receives and inspects your return.

## What if I received the wrong item?

Contact support within 48 hours. We ship the correct item immediately with free
express shipping and email you a prepaid label for the incorrect one. You are
never charged twice.

## Do products come with a warranty?

Yes — coverage varies by category: 1 year for electronics, 2 years for
appliances, 5 years for desk frames, 90 days for footwear, and a lifetime
warranty on SummitPack backpacks. See the Warranty Policy for details.

## Can I use more than one gift card on an order?

You can combine up to two payment methods per order, for example one gift card
plus one credit card. To use multiple gift cards, first merge their balances in
your account settings.

## Is my personal data shared with third parties?

We share data only with carriers (to deliver packages) and payment processors
(to charge your order). We never sell personal data. See our privacy notice for
details.

## How do I contact a human agent?

The support assistant can handle orders, returns, and product questions. For
anything else, email support@example-retail.com — replies within 1 business day.
