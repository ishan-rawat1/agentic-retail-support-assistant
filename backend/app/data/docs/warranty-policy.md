# Warranty Policy

## Coverage by category

- **Electronics** (Aurora Wireless Headphones, LumenGlow Smart Lamp): **1-year
  limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship.
- **Appliances** (EverBrew Coffee Maker): **2-year warranty** covering motors,
  heating elements, and electronic controls.
- **Furniture** (NimbusDesk Standing Desk): **5-year warranty on the frame** and
  **2-year warranty on motors and electronics**.
- **Outdoor gear** (SummitPack 45L Hiking Backpack): **lifetime warranty** against
  defects in stitching, zippers, and buckles.
- **Footwear** (TrailBlaze Running Shoes): **90-day warranty** against sole
  separation and manufacturing defects.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear, accidental damage, water damage to non-waterproof products,
unauthorized repairs or modifications, and damage from misuse are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim

1. Contact support with your order number and photos or a video of the defect.
2. Claims are reviewed within **2 business days**.
3. Approved claims receive a repair, an identical replacement, or store credit if
   the product has been discontinued. Warranty service is free, including return
   shipping for the defective unit.

The warranty period starts on the **delivery date**. Warranty claims do not
require the item to be within its return window — a warranty claim is different
from a return.
